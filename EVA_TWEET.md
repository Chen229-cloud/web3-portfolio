🧵 Most L2s are copies. @EvaFun is building something different.

Eva Mainnet isn't just another rollup — it's a chain purpose-built for onchain entertainment and social apps.

Here's why this matters 👇

1/ The problem with current L2s:

They're optimized for DeFi. High TPS, low fees — but zero native support for social primitives.

Content, communities, and entertainment apps are bolted on as afterthoughts. The chain doesn't "understand" them.

2/ Eva Mainnet flips this:

Built from the ground up for:
→ Onchain social graphs
→ Entertainment dApps (games, predictions, content)
→ Creator economies
→ Community-owned media

The EVM compatibility is there. But the native primitives are social-first.

3/ Why social needs its own chain:

Social apps have different requirements than DeFi:
- Frequent low-value transactions (likes, follows, tips)
- Identity and reputation systems
- Content storage and discovery
- Community governance at scale

A DeFi chain treats these as edge cases. Eva treats them as first-class citizens.

4/ The bigger picture:

We're moving from "money onchain" to "life onchain."

DeFi proved crypto works for money. Now we need infrastructure for everything else — social, entertainment, culture.

Eva Mainnet is betting on this next wave.
